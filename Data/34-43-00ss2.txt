t=There are two levels of advisories: TAs - Traffic Advisories indicate the range, bearing and relative altitude of the intruder to aid in visual acquisition of the intruder.  A TA is defined as an intruder aircraft entering the caution area, 35 to 45 seconds from the TCAS collision area. Note: A visual search is recommended to prepare for intruder avoidance. RAs - Resolution Advisories indicate what vertical maneuver must be performed or avoided to assure safe separation.  A RA is given for an intruder aircraft entering the warning area, 20 to 30 seconds from the TCAS collision area. Note: Prompt action is required to avoid the intruder. If the intruding aircraft is also equipped with TCAS, the two systems can communicate their mutual intentions for an avoidance manoeuvre through the Mode S transponders. Mode A transponder-equipped intruders result in detection and display of TAs only. Intruders not equipped with a transponder are invisible to the TCAS.
