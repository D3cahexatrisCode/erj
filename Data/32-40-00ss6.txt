t=The auto-brake module provides pressure commands to the BCMs if the auto-brake control is active. Auto-brake control can be selected on or off by the crew by means of the auto-brake selector switch in the cockpit. The following auto-brake modes can be selected: RTO, for automatic braking following a rejected take-off; LO, MED or HI, for three different levels of deceleration after landing. During landing auto-brake application, the deceleration rate is calculated by the ABM using changes in ground speed. The crew can re-select the deceleration to any of the three levels without disarming the auto-brake. If the brake control system detects a fault, the autobrake selector switch springs back to the OFF position. The following BCM protections are still available during auto-brake control: anti-skid control, touchdown protection and locked wheel protection.
