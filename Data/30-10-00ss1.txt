t=The EMBRAER 170 wing anti-ice system is a thermal anti-ice system that provides ice protection for the slats outboard of the engines (slats 2, 3, and 4) to allow the aircraft to safely operate in icing conditions. The wing anti-ice system utilizes engine bleed air to heat the slat surfaces to avoid ice accretion. The required skin temperature for the respective flight condition is calculated within the AMS controller. A wing anti-ice valve then modulates the flow of the engine bleed air to the slat surfaces to achieve the required skin temperature. The system is activated automatically by the aircraft ice detection system whenever icing conditions exist, and shuts off automatically when no longer needed. System status is displayed to the pilots on the EICAS and on the MFD anti-ice synoptic page.
