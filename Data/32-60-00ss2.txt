t=The EICAS indicates of the Landing Gear using three colored symbols. From left to right, each box represents the position of the left, nose and right landing gear. With the landing gear down and locked, green circles enclosing a green word "DOWN" are indicated. If the control lever is moved and the gears are in transition, amber cross hatches are shown. If the landing gear is up and locked, white squares enclosing a white word "UP" are displayed. A disagreement between the control lever position and any landing gear leg position for more than 20 seconds will activate the message LG LEVER DISAG and the indication of the respective landing gear will turn red. If at least one of the NLG doors fails to close following gear retraction, or one of the two forward doors fails to close after extension, the LG NOSE DOOR OPEN caution message will be displayed on the EICAS.
