t=The flood lights also called storm lights provide sufficient lighting intensity to illuminate the main cockpit panel and prevent cabin crew blinding during lightning conditions. The flood lights located under the main panel are controlled by an on/off variable intensity control knob on the glare shield panel. The pilot's PFD, MFD and EICAS display brightness controls are located on the left glare shield panel, while the co-pilot's PFD, MFD and  standby instruments brightness controls are located from the right glare shield panel.
