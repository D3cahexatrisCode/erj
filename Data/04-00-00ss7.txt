t=The Embraer 170 has a forward retracting twin-wheel nose landing gear. The nose landing gear has a normal steering angle of  up-to 76° either way. The shock absorbers are of oleo-pneumatic type. The steering motor, taxi light and one landing light are mounted on the nose landing gear. When the nose landing gear is extended, the rear doors remain open while the front doors re-close after extension or retraction. The main landing gears also have oleo -pneumatic shock absorbers and twin wheels and retract sideways.
