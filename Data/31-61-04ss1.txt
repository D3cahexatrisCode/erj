t=The EICAS will be decluttered if the following conditions are met: The aircraft is flying in a clean configuration (flap and gear up) for more than 30 seconds and none of the decluttered parameters have been exceeded. If these conditions are met, the following items are decluttered during cruise: Oil pressure, Oil temperature, Low pressure vibration, High pressure vibration, Gear position, Flap position, Slat position, Speed brake position and on the pitch trim scale the green takeoff band will extinguish. Pushing the EICAS full button displays all of the decluttered items.
