t=The APU fire detection system receives electrical power from the Essential DC Buses via the MAUs. There are two loops with one detector each, protecting the same area. If both loops detect a fire condition during normal operation, the MAUs will generate an “APU FIRE” warning message in the cockpit, and the red striped bar of the APU emergency stop button will be illuminated.  When pressed, this button will shut down the APU immediately, the red illumination will turn OFF, illuminating the white striped bar of this button, and the APU fire extinguisher button will illuminate.
