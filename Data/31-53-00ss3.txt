t=To inform the flight crew regarding aircraft system status, EICAS messages are presented in the upper right corner of the EICAS display in a box know as the CAS field. There are four message levels: warning, caution, advisory and status. Warning (red colored) is used to indicate emergency conditions of an aircraft system or aircraft operation that require immediate corrective or compensatory crew action. Caution (amber colour) is used to indicate abnormal conditions of an aircraft system or operation that require immediate crew awareness and subsequent corrective or compensatory crew action. Advisory (cyan colour) is used to indicate operational or aircraft conditions that require crew awareness and may require subsequent or future crew action. Status (white colour) is used to indicate information or status messages. That means, when the appropriate action is taken, the pilot can make efficient use of the system.
