t=If the aural LANDING GEAR warning is triggered as a result of an invalid radio altimeter signal, the pilots may cancel the warning by pressing the Landing Gear Warning Inhibition switch in the cockpit. When pressed, a white bar within the switch indicates a cancelled warning. Advancing the thrust levers above TLA 45° or TLA 59°, respectively, or selecting the flaps to landing configuration, or when all landing gear are down and locked will extinguish the white indication.
