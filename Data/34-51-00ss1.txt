t=The Distance Measuring Equipment system (DME) is an integrated part of the EPIC avionics suite. The DME system enables en route and terminal navigation and area guidance. The DME is capable of tracking four channels to provide slant range, time to station, and station identification. Frequency tuning is automatically paired with VHF NAV. The DME can also operate in the HOLD ON mode in which case the frequency is not paired with VHF NAV. When only one DME is is installed, one station ident channel is for the Captain's active frequency and the other for the Co pilot's active frequency. The DME system consists of DME module and a DME antenna (a second is optional). The DME interfaces with the display system and FMS through the ASCB-D bus.
