t=The cabin pressure control system operates for aircraft ceilings up to 41'000 ft. During cruise below 37'000 ft the nominal differential pressure will be limited to 7.8 PSID, while cruising above 37'000 ft will limit the differential pressure to 8.3 PSID. As a result, maximum cabin altitude at aircraft ceiling is limited to 8'000 ft. A cabin altitude high warning is generated whenever the cabin altitude rises above 9'700 ft. Note: In case of high airfield operation, the warning will be active for a cabin altitude of 500 ft above take off field elevation. For cabin altitudes exceeding 14'500 ft, the outflow valve will close automatically to prevent the cabin from climbing further. The system automatically limits differential pressure by opening the outflow valve , or in the event of failure, by opening the safety valve.
