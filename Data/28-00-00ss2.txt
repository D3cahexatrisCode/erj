t=Fuel is carried in two integral wing tanks. The aircraft fuel capacity is 9496 kg (21102 lbs), or 4748 kg (10551 lbs) per tank. During normal operation, each wing tank supplies fuel to one engine through a collector tank, located at the inboard end of each tank. The APU is supplied with fuel from the right hand side collector tank. The engines are fed by means of primary ejector pumps, which are driven by high-pressure motive fuel flow supplied by the dedicated engine fuel pumps. The APU is fed by means of electrical pumps when the engines are not running, or by means of the primary ejector pumps with the engines running. The backup electrical pumps in each collector tank ensure engine feed in the event of a primary ejector pump failure, and also provide a source of pressurized fuel supply to the engines and APU for ground and in-flight start.
