t=The two wing-mounted CF34-8E high bypass turbofan engines are based on the CF34 engine family, which is widely used in aviation. Engine controls and fuel scheduling are provided by a full-authority digital electronic control (FADEC) with fully modular design. The CF34-8E incorporates the aerodynamic efficiency of wide cord fan, which produces most of the engine's 14,200 Lbs maximum thrust. To enhance aircraft braking capability, the engines are able to reverse thrust.
