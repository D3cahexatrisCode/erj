t=To establish communication, the pilot pushes the CABIN button on the Audio Control Panel. The display on the audio control panel shows CABIN CALL, and the CABIN pushbutton light flashes until the flight attendant picks up the call. If another audio panel is talking on the Cabin interphone, the display shows BUSY. The microphone signal is routed to the cabin interphone stations, a chime in the cabin sounds and the cabin annunciator illuminates. When established, the display shows the characters CABIN plus a number between 0 and 100 denoting the volume. If the pilot selects another microphone pushbutton, the CABIN select push button remains lit. To return to the cabin, the pushbutton must be pushed again. For an incoming call, the CABIN pushbutton light flashes and a chime sounds. To pick-up the call, the pilot pushes the CABIN pushbutton. At this time, its light stays on and the display shows the characters CABIN plus a number between 0 and 100 denoting the volume. After the call is completed the CABIN button is pushed again, the light extinguishes and the display shows the last active radio.
