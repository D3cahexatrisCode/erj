t=The auxiliary power generator is the secondary power source on ground and in abnormal operation in flight. It is mounted on the auxiliary power unit. With the APU at constant speed, the generator supplies constant frequency AC power. The generator provides 40 kVA, 115 V AC, 400 Hz, 3 - phase. The lubrication system shares the oil cooling system with the APU. The APU generator switch on the cockpit overhead panel provides control of the auxiliary generator, and must be pushed in during normal, automatic operation. If the switch is pushed out, the auxiliary generator line contactor opens, the auxiliary generator trips off line and is de-excited. A bar illuminates inside the button when it is pushed out.
