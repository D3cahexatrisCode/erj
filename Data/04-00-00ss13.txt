t=The crew emergency windows are located in the cockpit on the left and right hand sides. When closed, they are pushed against the window frame by an over centre mechanism and held in position by a locking mechanism. The windows are opened manually. They have a locking mechanism that is released by pressing the button located on the top of the handle. As the window is unlocked, the handle must be pulled backward in order to actuate the rollers latching mechanism sliding the window inside and backward along the tracks. The red indicator is pulled out of its housing to indicate the window is not fully closed. A POSITION LOCK SYSTEM permits the window to stop in an intermediate position and does not allow closure unless the lever lock is moved into the “close window" position. In an emergency, when leaving the aircraft by the doors is impossible, the crew can open the windows and exit by two ropes which are stowed above the windows.
