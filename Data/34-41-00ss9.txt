t=Some buttons in the cockpit allow pilots to interact with the EGPWS. The respective buttons are: The EGPWS TERRAIN SYSTEM INHIBIT BUTTON - when pressed, in approach mode it inhibits EGPWS and  avoids unwanted terrain alerts in airports not covered by EGPWS database. The EGPWS GLIDE SLOPE INHIBIT BUTTON – the momentary action button annunciator is used to manually cancel glide slope alerts. It illuminates when pressed any time below 2,000 ft radar altitude and will be automatically reset (light off) by climbing above 2,000 ft or descending below 30 ft. The FLAP OVERRIDE SWITCH (GUARDED) - Inhibits triggering flap alerts in case of landings in which flap configuration is different from flap landing configuration.
