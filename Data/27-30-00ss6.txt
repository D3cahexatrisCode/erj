t=The Elevator Thrust Compensation function (ETC) helps to reduce the pilot workload by applying elevator commands to reduce the pitching moment produced by increasing or decreasing engine thrust. The ETC function is computed in the FCM as a function of N1, mach and pressure altitude. Elevator command is limited to plus or minus 5 degrees, and is applied proportional to the amount of engine thrust above or below the reference thrust setting. The command is sent to the P-ACE units and is then summed with the normal pilot input. If one or more sensors required to perform the ETC function fail, the function is no longer available, and the respective message will be displayed on the EICAS.
