t=Landing auto-brake control can be “armed” by the crew under the following conditions: the auto-brake selector switch is set to LO, MED or HI; left and right WOW indicate that the aircraft is in flight; the average wheel speed is below 60 knots; brake pedals are not pressed in; and no brake control system failures are detected. Landing auto-brake control will be applied after touchdown when the following conditions apply: Landing auto-brake is “armed” and the average wheel speed is above 60 knots; either WOW indicates aircraft on ground for more than 2 seconds; and both thrust levers are in idle position. Landing auto-brake will be disarmed if: the selector switch is set to OFF; the brake pedals are pressed in; any failure in the brake control system is detected; or either throttle lever is advanced beyond idle.
