t=The CAS messages are grouped and presented in chronological order according to their category. The warning category is at the top of the CAS field. Below this category, the system presents the Caution, Advisory and Status messages. When new Warning, Caution and Advisory alert messages are displayed, they are presented flashing in inverse video before acknowledgment. The warning and caution alert messages remain flashing in inverse video until manual crew acknowledgment via Master Warning or Caution button (respectively) is pressed. After the acknowledgment, the messages remain in steady normal video, at the top of their respective category on the CAS field, until a new message belonging to that group appears. Advisory messages will be automatically acknowledged after 5 seconds when they become normal video, until a new message belonging to that group appears. A white END Message, centered in the line, is displayed following the last message.
