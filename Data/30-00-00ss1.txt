t=The EMBRAER 170 Ice and Rain protection system includes the following subsystems: The engine anti-ice system and the wing anti-ice system, which use bleed air to provide the thermal energy used to heat the respective surfaces to avoid ice accumulation. The Air Data Smart Probe heating system, which uses electrical power to heat the Smart probes and the TAT probes for continued sensor accuracy in icing environments. The Windshield heating system uses electrical power to prevent ice accretion on the exterior surface and fog formation on the interior surface of the windshields. The Windshield Wiper system protects the windshields mechanically to allow safe flight in the rain. The Water and Waste ice protection system uses electrical power to prevent the aircrafts water and waste system from freezing. Finally, the Ice Detection system signals icing condition to other aircraft systems in order to automatically activate the engine and wing anti-ice systems, and to advise flight crew about ice conditon.
