t=The MFD status page has two ways of providing temperature indication: numerical and pictorial. There are two brake temperature categories on the MFD: status and advisory. Temperature indication is green up to 251°C, which means that the brakes are able to absorb maximum kinetic energy. Advisory indication is shown in amber between 252°C and 850°C, which indicates that cooling is required. If the temperature information has failed or is out of range, the arrow will disappear and the readout will be replaced by amber dashes. The BRAKE OVERHEAT caution message will illuminate on the EICAS when the brake temperatures has exceeded the normal operatin range. The message will disappear if the temperature drops below a predetermined value. If the brake temperature reaches 750°C, the advisory message LG TEMP EXCEEDANCE will latch and can only be unlatched by electrically powering down the airplane. Note that the fuse plugs will melt in the main wheel, and inspections, according to the Maintenance Manual, have to be performed.
