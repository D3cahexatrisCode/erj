t=A three-position rotary selector knob located on the cockpit control panel controls the crossfeed valve and associated AC Fuel Pump, if the AC Fuel Pump selector knobs are in the AUTO position. If an AC Fuel Pump selector knob is in the OFF or ON position, this command will override the crossfeed command. With the XFEED selector knob in the OFF position, the crossfeed valve remains closed. Selecting the selector knob to LOW 1 or LOW 2 commands the crossfeed valve to open. When the XFEED selector knob is selected to LOW 1 (the fuel quantity in tank 1 is lower than in tank 2), AC Fuel Pump 2 will be powered to feed fuel from tank 2 to tank 1. When the XFEED selector knob is selected to LOW 2 position (the fuel quantity in tank 2 is lower than in tank 1), AC Fuel Pump 1 will be powered to feed fuel from tank 1 to tank 2. Note: Crossfeed operation is not to be used during takeoff or landing.
