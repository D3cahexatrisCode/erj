The autothrottle can be engaged on the ground when:
• All parameters required are valid and autothrottle is capable.
• Autothrottle TO mode armed
• Both thrust leves above 50° TLA.

In flight, the autothrottle can be engaged when:
• All parameters required are valid and autothrottle is capable.
• Autothrottle button in the guidance panel is pressed.
• The airplane is above 400 ft AGL.

The auto throttle system has the following modes:
• Speed Control Mode – Speed on Thrust (SPDT).
• Flight Level Change Thrust Control Mode – Speed on Elevator
(SPDE)
• Takeoff Thrust Control Mode (TO)
• Go-Around Thrust Control Mode (GA)
• Takeoff Thrust Hold Mode (HOLD)
• Retard Mode
