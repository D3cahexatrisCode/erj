Disconnection of AT occurs when:
• Either AT Disconnect Button on the thrust levers is pressed.
• AT button on the GP is pressed.
• TLA difference greater than 8°.
• AT monitor tripped.
• The required system parameters become invalid.
• Transition to on-ground condition (weight-on-wheels or wheels spinning), and thrust levers at Idle and AT in Retard mode.

Pressing the AT Disconnect Button manually disconnects the autothrottle. 
Anytime the auto Throttle is deactivated, an aural alarm “THROTLLE” is triggered and the autothrottle message on the FMA will flash for at least 5 seconds.
Pressing the Disconnect Button a second time cancels the visual and aural alarm, which sounds at least once.

During an abnormal disconnection of the autothrottle, the aural alarm “THROTLLE” is also triggered, a red autothrottle message on the FMA will start to flash on the FMA and an EICAS message will be displayed.
Pressing the Disconnect Button a second time cancels the visual and aural alarm, which sounds at least once.
Pressing the Disconnect Button will cancel the visual and aural alarm, but the EICAS message will remain displayed.
