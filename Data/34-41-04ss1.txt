t=Dangerous shear winds can result from vertical winds and rapidly changing horizontal winds. Therefore, special shear wind advisories have to be given to the pilots. Windshear warning alerts are given for decreasing head wind, increasing tail wind and severe vertical down drafts. Windshear caution alerts are given for increasing head wind and severe updrafts. The Windshear (WSHR) system is part of the Flight Guidance Control System (FGCS) and Enhanced Ground Proximity Warning System (EGPWS). The EGPWS provides the detection portion of the windshear system and the FGCS provides the guidance portion.
