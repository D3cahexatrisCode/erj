t=The windshield is constructed of laminated glass. The outboard ply is coated with an anti-static film to provide a discharge mechanism for static buildup, which helps to prevent damage to the heating system. The windshield heater is a film located on the inboard surface of the outer glass ply, which provides anti-ice capability. There are three sensing elements - two connected to the Windshield Heater Control Unit and one spare.
