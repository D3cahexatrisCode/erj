t=The ice protection panel in the cockpit allows the pilots to control the engine anti-ice system using the MODE selector knob, the ENGINE 1 and 2 buttons and the TEST selector knob. Selecting the MODE selector knob to the AUTO position enables the system to be activated whenever the ice detectors detect ice. The engine anti-ice system can also be manually activated by the pilots by selecting the MODE selector knob to the ON position. In both cases the ENGINE Anti Ice Valve will modulate to achieve the required airflow to the inlet D-duct. Note that the MODE selector knob also controls the wing anti-ice system. With the ENGINE 1 and 2 buttons in the “pushed in” position, both engine anti-ice valves operate either in AUTO or ON mode, as selected by the pilot. Selecting either of the ENGINE 1 or 2 buttons to the “pushed out” position manually drives the engine anti-ice valves to the closed position, shutting down the respective side engine anti-ice system regardless of the mode selector knob position.
