t=Rejected Take-Off (RTO) auto-brake can be “armed” by the crew under the following conditions:
the auto-brake selector switch is set to RTO;
the left and right WOW indicate that the aircraft is on ground;
the average wheel speed is below 60 knots;
and no brake control system failures are detected.
RTO auto-brake control will be applied during a rejected take-off: if RTO auto-brake is armed and the average wheel speed is above 60 knots, and both thrust levers are in idle position.
RTO auto-brake will be disarmed if:
the selector switch is set to OFF,
any braking is applied while wheel speed is above 60 knots;
any brake control system failure is detected while wheel speed is below 60 knots;
or either thrust lever is advanced beyond idle.
