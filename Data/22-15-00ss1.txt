t=During execution of an ILS approach, the Autopilot Approach Status Annunciator displays the current status of the system and alerts whether the intended approach matches the system capabilities. When ILS modes are requested via APP button, system arms for the highest capability available and the RA/BARO selector and RA Minimums setting inform the system of what is the intended approach. For CAT1 – set RA/BARO selector to BARO (both sides). For CAT2 – set both RA/BARO selectors to RA and adjust Minimums to 80ft or above. If all necessary requirements are not accomplished, an EICAS message is presented during flight to inform that category II ILS approach mode is not available.
