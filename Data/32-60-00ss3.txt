t=The landing gear warning system will provide a "LANDING GEAR" voice message to the pilots if at least on gear is not locked down and there is an intention to land the aircraft. The landing gear aural warning logic uses the thrust lever angle TLA, flap setting and the radio altimeter to define the intention to land the aircraft. When the aircraft is flying below 1200 ft AGL and any of the thrust levers are reduced to below 45° TLA with one or more landing gears not locked down, the aural warning "LANDING GEAR" will sound. During single engine operation, TLA 59° will trigger the warning for the conditions mentioned. If the flaps are set to one of the two landing positions with at least one landing gear not locked down, the warning will be triggered independent of altitude or TLA position. Note: In case of partial gear-up during landing, follow the appropriate Checklist.
