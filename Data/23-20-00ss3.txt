t=To establish an emergency call, the pilot has to press the EMER button. In the cabin, this initiates a triple chime and the cabin annunciator illuminates. In the same manner when the flight attendant pushes EMER on the cabin interphone station, the pilot will hear a triple chime and see a flashing EMER button on the ACP panel. To pick up the call the procedure is the same as for the cabin interphone.
