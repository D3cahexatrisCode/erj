t=The left and right fuel quantities can be monitored on the EICAS. The fuel system synoptic page can be selected on the MFD by selecting "systems" and then "fuel". Total fuel quantity and fuel used are displayed as digital values. In addition to digital values, the left and right tank quantities are shown on an analog scale. The fuel temperature in the left hand tank is displayed in green as long as the temperature is above -40°C. Below that, it appears in amber and amber dashes if there is a fault. The status of the main ejector and electrical pumps is indicated in white as long as they are in standby; turn green when in operation and amber dashes if there is a fault.
