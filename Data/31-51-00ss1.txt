t=Aural warnings are used when the pilots require immediate knowledge of the condition without having to look at a visual display or indicator. Aural warnings are “alert tones,” like the single chime and the  triple chime, “bells” to indicate an engine or APU fire or an cargo smoke detection and  “voice messages” used in systems like TCAS and EPGWS. Aural warnings are canceled automatically when the alerting situation no longer exists, or when they are reset manually by the pilot. In the event of multiple alerts, the priority in order of highest to lowest is: EGPWS, TCAS, fire, overspeed and landing gear. Aural alerts sound in a sequence, and are never truncated.
