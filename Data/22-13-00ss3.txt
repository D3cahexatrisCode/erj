t=HEADING SELECT (HDG). The heading select mode activates under the following conditions: The Heading pushbutton on the Guidance Panel is pressed. The LNAV, LOCALIZER or Back Course modes are armed. The Heading Select mode is deselected when: The Heading pushbutton is pressed a second time. By selecting a different lateral mode, or when LNAV, LOCALIZER or Back Course modes are activated. Pressing the HDG selector knob synchronizes the heading bug to the current heading. The FD follows the selected heading and respects the side to which the turn was commanded, regardless of turn being greater than 180 degrees.
