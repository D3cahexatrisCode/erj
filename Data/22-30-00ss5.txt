SPEED CONTROL MODE – SPEED ON THRUST (SPDT)
In this mode the thrust levers are commanded to provide a thrust rate as programmed to maintain the desired speed.
The selected speed is controlled by engine thrust during climb, descend and cruise phases.
The vertical modes related to SPDT mode are:
• Flight Path Angle (FPA) – basic vertical mode
• Vertical Speed (VS)
• Glide Slope (GS)
• Altitude Hold (ALT)
• Altitude Select (ASEL)
