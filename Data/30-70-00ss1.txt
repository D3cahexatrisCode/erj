t=The EMBRAER 170 also incorporates ice protection features to prevent its water and waste system from freezing. The water lines ice protection system includes forward and rear drain mast heating to prevent hazardous ice formation during draining of water, and a cuff heater installed on the fill/drain nipple of the service panel to prevent clogging of the line from water freezing in the fill line. The waste service panel ice protection system includes gasket type heaters installed under the drain valve and rinse nipple on the waste service panel to prevent ice formation during flight.
