t=The system can be operated either in the active mode, once the handwheel has been pushed down, or in the free-wheel steering mode. In the active mode, the flight crew actively steers the aircraft by means of the handwheel rotation, keeping it pressed in down position, or by the rudder pedals, if the NLG is on the ground and the NWSCM detects no failure. In the free-wheel steering mode, the NLG steers, in response to external forces like towing or differential braking and is enabled, as soon as the steering has been disengaged, using the pilot disarm switch or via the outside steering switch to DISENGAGE position for ground handling. STEER OFF will be displayed on the EICAS. The free-wheel steering mode is also automatically engaged any time the actual steering angle is greater than 76 degrees. If the crew switches from the free-wheel steering mode to the normal mode, the steering command from the NWSCM is gradually changed from the actual steering angle to the handwheel or rudder pedal command angle.
