t=In regard to the aircraft anti-ice systems, the crew configures the aircraft for take-off depending on weather conditions by selecting the Take-off Data Set (TDS) and the ICE PROTECTION PANEL. This information enables or inhibits both the engine and wing anti-ice systems during take-off, and communicates to the FADECs whether or not bleed air will be needed to feed the anti-ice systems during the take-off roll. TDS menu settings will be disregarded by the aircraft as soon as the aircraft reaches 1700 ft altitude above the field, or 2 minutes after lift-off, whichever is sooner. For the remainder of the flight, the wing and engine anti-ice systems will be activated any time icing conditions are detected by the ice detectors, or the MODE switch is set to the ON  position. Therefore, no inhibition in the activation of the anti-ice systems will occur during climb, cruise, approach, landing and go-around.
