t=FGCS VERTICAL MODES
Only one vertical mode can be active at any time and up to two vertical modes can be armed at one time.
The FGCS provides the following vertical navigation modes:
FLIGHT PATH ANGLE (FPA)
TAKEOFF (TO)
ALTITUDE SELECT (ASEL)
FLIGHT LEVEL CHANGE (FLCH)
ALTITUDE HOLD (ALT)
VERTICAL SPEED (VS)
OVERSPEED PROTECTION (OVSP)
GLIDE SLOPE (GS)
GO-AROUND (GA)
