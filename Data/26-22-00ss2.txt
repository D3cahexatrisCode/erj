t=When the guarded APU emergency stop button is pressed, the APU fuel shutoff valve will be commanded to close and the APU will shut down. Shutoff valve position is displayed on the EICAS. After the APU emergency stop button has been released, the APU shutoff valve can be re-opened by rotating the APU master switch to the ON position. In the event of an APU fire, by pressing the APU emergency stop button, the APU will stop and the fuel shutoff valve will close. To discharge the fire-extinguishing agent into the APU fire zone, the APU fire-extinguishing button should be pressed. Note: By pressing the APU fire extinguishing button the APU will shut down and the APU fuel shutoff valve will close, when the APU emergency stop button has not been pressed before.
