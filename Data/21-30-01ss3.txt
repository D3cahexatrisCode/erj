t=The outflow valve modulates the cabin pressure by modulating airflow from the pressurized cabin into the surrounding environment. It can be controlled automatically or manually. The system also includes a pneumatically-driven safety valve, which opens independently whenever differential pressure exceeds 8.6 PSID, to protect the aircraft structure against damages due to a positive overpressure A spring loaded negative relief valve limits the negative differential pressure to -0.5 PSID to protect the cabin against damage. The static pressure port senses the ambient static pressure and transmits it to the safety valve in order to allow the overpressure relief device to work. The static port is electrically heated in order to assure there are no obstructions of sensing orifices due to icing.
