t=The landing gear position and warning system is used to provide landing gear status and failure information. The system has the following subsystems: the Landing Gear Indicating System, the Air/Ground or Weight on Wheel system, and the Landing Gear Warning System. The Landing Gear Indicating system is controlled by two identical Proximity Sensor Electronics Modules, called PSEM, installed in MAU 1 and MAU 3. The PSEMs each make an analysis of the proximity sensors for the landing gear system, ground spoiler system and aircraft doors. The Air/Ground system makes use of proximity sensors on the MLG and NLG to determine whether the aircraft is on the ground or in flight. The landing gear warning system forwards failure information to the EICAS, and warns the pilots of any abnormal landing gear configuration during landing.
