t=The left and right pilot and copilot pedals control the left and right brakes independently during normal operation, allowing the pilots to steer the aircraft using differential braking. The brake pedal LVDTs transmit brake pedal position to the BCMs. The BCMs use closed loop electronic control to calculate a pressure command for each MLG wheel brake, relative to the pedal position. The BCMs add several high-level functions to optimise braking performance during various conditions: Anti-skid control reduces the pressure command to any wheel by comparing wheel speed signals from each wheel, in order to prevent wheel skid on slippery runways. Antiskid protection is active when wheel speed is above 10 kts. Touchdown protection inhibits a pressure command for any wheel if the aircraft is in flight, or when the aircraft is on ground for less than 3 seconds. However, a spin-up override function allows braking when wheelspeed is above 50 Kts even when touchdown protection is still active.
