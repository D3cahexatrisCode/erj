t=The following short introduction will give you a general overview of the Embraer 170. The Embraer 170 is a low wing, twin engine jet airplane of conventional structure, designed for medium to short range operations. The aircraft has a total length of 29.90 meters (98 ft 1 in.) , a wing span of 26 meters (85 ft 4 in.) and an approximate height of  9.82 meters (32 ft 3 in.) . It also features a pressurized cabin. The fuselage has a so-called double bubble design.
