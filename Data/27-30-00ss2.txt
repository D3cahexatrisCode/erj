t=The elevator control system uses conventional control column assemblies to drive the fly-by-wire elevator control system.  A total of four P-ACE channels are used to independently control each of the four PCUs, providing the analog elevator control functions implemented in the P-ACE channels, such as column shaping, to vary the control column-to-surface gearing as a function of flap / slat position. Four independent FCM units, located in MAU 1 and 3, provide high level system augmentation to the P-ACE channels, such as gain scheduling as a function of airspeed, elevator thrust compensation and AOA (Angle-Off-Attack) limiting.
