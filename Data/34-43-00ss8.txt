t=Traffic advisories and resolution advisories are always displayed on TCAS map overlay or TCAS zoom formats. When the TCAS detects an RA condition where certain ranges of vertical speed are not recommended (preventive RA) one red trapezoidal avoidance zone is displayed. When the TCAS detects an RA condition and a maneuver is recommended to increase the vertical separation (corrective RA) one or two red trapezoidal avoidance zones and a green rectangular “fly-to-zone” are displayed. For any RA condition the Flight Director bars are removed and the Flight Path Angle (FPA) will become RED when it is within the avoidance zone, otherwise it remains GREEN. The resolution advisories are also displayed on Vertical Speed Indicator (VSI).
