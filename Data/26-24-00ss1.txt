t=To fight a fire on board, the crew can make use of the portable fire extinguishers installed in the cabin and cockpit areas. Each fire extinguisher contains a blended Halon 1211/1301 with low toxicity characteristics. One fire extinguisher is located on the left hand side of the cockpit bulkhead, behind the pilot seat and there are fire extinguishers located in each galley area. The standard configuration will be equipped with 2.5 lb Halon.
