t=The seats must be adjusted to suit the pilot's optimum viewing position.  To accomplish this, the seat has to be moved up or down until the pilot's line of sight reaches the horizontal plane of the black and white factory- adjusted ball indicators. When the horizontal plane of the ball indicators is reached, the seat must be moved fore or aft until the white ball indicator becomes aligned to the black ball indicator. Once the optimum seat position is found, the pedals can then be adjusted through the pedal adjustment switch.
