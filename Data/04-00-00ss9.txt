t=The general cockpit interior provides an ergonomic working space for two pilots, one observer, and also storage of working and emergency equipment. The cockpit is configured as follows: Two pilot seats; A foldable flight observer seat positioned just in front of the cockpit door. When not in use, this seat stows behind the pilot seat; A cockpit bulkhead with a lockable door; Two Control columns and rudder pedals; Crew emergency exits on direct vision windows on each side of the cockpit; A main instrument panel protected by a glare shield; Control pedestal located between the two pilot seats; Overhead panel located in the centre of the ceiling panels; Lateral consoles located beside each pilot seat; Aft right hand lateral console also called observer console.
